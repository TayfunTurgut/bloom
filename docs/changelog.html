Version 1.1

31 August 2026

New

  • Music and sound effects, each with its own on/off switch in Settings.
  • A guided first level that walks you through planting, feeding and harvesting.
  • Rocks appear on later levels — harvest a neighbour to break one free.
  • A redesigned Settings window with Skins and Credits tabs, and a highlight on the crop you'll place next.

Better

  • Place crops back to back without waiting for animations.
  • The top bar now stays clear of the notch and Dynamic Island on every iPhone.
  • Tidier, smaller boards on the early levels.

Your progress and skins carry over.
