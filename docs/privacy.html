Privacy Policy Sprout

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Sprout is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version: Sprout does not collect any data about you.

Information we collect

None. Sprout does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no logins, and no sign-up.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — the level you have reached, your leaves, and your chosen crop skin — is saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device, is not linked to your identity, and is removed if you delete the app.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

Sprout contains no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs. The app does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across apps or websites.

Third parties

Sprout does not share information with third parties, because it does not collect any.

Children’s privacy

Sprout is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email support.sprout@gmail.com.
