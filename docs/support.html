Frequently asked

How do I play?

Tap an empty tile to plant a crop. A crop with no neighbours starts growing. Plant next to a growing crop to feed it — feed it down to zero to harvest it. Harvest the crops shown at the top before the board fills up.

What does the swap button do?

It swaps the last two crops in your tray, for free, so you can plan your next placement.

Is my progress saved?

Yes — your progress is saved automatically on your device. It stays on that device and is removed if you delete the app.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. Sprout is a one-time purchase with no ads and no in-app purchases.
